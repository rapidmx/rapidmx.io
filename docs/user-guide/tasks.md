# Tasks

Tasks is a simple to-do list that lives in your mailbox, so it is the same in the web app and on your phone. Add something you need to do, give it a due date and a priority, and tick it off when it's done. Messages you flag in Mail show up here too.

Open it with **Tasks** in the app rail, or press `Ctrl+Shift+L`.

## Views

The list on the left shows your tasks in different ways:

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **My Day** | Tasks you picked for today. |
| **Important** | Tasks with high priority. |
| **Planned** | Tasks with a due date. |
| **Assigned to me** | Tasks someone else assigned to you. |
| **Flagged email** | Messages you flagged in Mail. |
| **Tasks** | All your tasks. |
| **Lists** | Lists you created. |

Tasks are grouped by when they're due: **Overdue**, **Today**, **This Week**, **Later** and **No due date**, with **Completed** tasks at the end. **Grid** shows them as a table with **Title**, **Due Date** and **Importance** columns instead; **List** goes back.

## Add a task

1. Type the task in **Add a task** at the top (or press `Alt+N` to jump there).
2. Optionally pick a **Due date** and a **Priority**: **Low**, **Normal** or **High**.
3. Click **Add**.

A task added here goes into **Tasks**, not into the list or My Day you're looking at.

To add a task with more detail, use Calendar: click **+ New event**, choose the **Task** tab, and click **More options**. There you can also set a due time, pick a **Task list**, add **Notes**, a reminder (**Add reminder**) and **Add to My Day**.

A task's details can't be changed after it is created yet. Delete it and add it again if you need to.

## Complete or delete tasks

- Click the circle next to a task to mark it complete. Click it again to undo.
- Click **×** on a task, or tick several and click **Delete**, then confirm.
- Tick several tasks to **Complete** them or **Add to My Day** all at once.

## Lists

Click **+** next to **Lists**, type a name and click **Add**. Lists can't be renamed or deleted in the web app yet.

## Flagged email

Flag a message in Mail (**More actions** > **Flag**, or press `Insert`) and it appears under **Flagged email** with its subject, sender and date. Unflag it in Mail when you're done.
