# Calendar

Calendar holds your appointments and meetings. When you invite colleagues, they get an invitation in their mail and their answers come back to you; you can see when they're free before you pick a time, and book a meeting room in the same step. Calendars of mailboxes that are shared with you, such as a team mailbox, appear next to your own.

Open it with **Calendar** in the app rail, or press `Ctrl+Shift+C`.

## Move around the calendar

- **Change the view** with the buttons at the top: **List**, **Month**, **Week**, **Work Week**, **Day** and **Split**. **Split** puts each of your calendars side by side for one day. (**Work Week** and **Split** aren't offered on a phone.) The app remembers your choice on each device.
- **Move in time** with **‹** and **›**, or go back with **Today**. On a phone, swipe left or right.
- **Jump to a date** with the small month calendar on the left. In **Month** view, click a day's number to open that day.
- **Show or hide a calendar** with the check box next to its name in the list on the left.

## Create an event

1. Click **+ New event**, or click an empty time in the calendar. (In **Month** view, clicking an empty part of a day starts an all-day event.)
2. In the small window that opens, type a title in **Add title**.
3. Click the date and time to change them, or to make the event repeat.
4. Fill in what you need: **Add guests**, video conferencing, **Add location**, **Add description**.
5. Click **Save**.

For everything else, click **More options** to open the full form. It adds:

- **Time zone**, to set the event in another time zone, and **All day**.
- **Add notification**: a reminder, 30 minutes before unless you change it. An event has one reminder.
- **Busy status**: **Busy**, **Free**, **Tentative** or **Out of office**.
- **Visibility**: **Default visibility**, **Public**, **Private** or **Confidential**. A private event shows to others who can see your calendar only as a "Busy" block.
- **Send an automatic reply while this event is happening**, with its own message, for example during a day off. See [Automatic replies](rules-and-automatic-replies.md).
- **Guest permissions** (see below).
- **Mailbox** and **Calendar**, when you can add events to more than one calendar.

Drag an event to move it, or drag its lower edge to change its length. On a touch screen, press and hold first.

### Make an event repeat

In the date and time row, open the repeat menu and pick **Daily**, **Weekly on** a weekday, **Monthly**, **Annually**, **Every weekday (Monday to Friday)** or **Custom…**. With **Custom…** you set **Every** so many days, weeks, months or years, the weekdays, and when it **Ends**: **Never**, **After** a number of occurrences, or **On** a date.

### Add a video meeting

Tick **Add video conferencing** (you need at least one guest besides yourself). After you save, the event shows **Join video call** and a link to copy. This needs the video meetings plugin; see [Video meetings](video-meetings.md). If you change the guests afterwards, turn video conferencing off and on again to send links to the new guest list.

## Invite people and find a time

1. In **Add guests**, start typing a name or address. Suggestions come from your contacts and your organization's directory. A group is added as one guest.
2. In the full form, choose each guest's role: **required** or **optional**.
3. Click **Find a time** (in the full form) to see everyone's busy times for the day. Use **Previous day** and **Next day** to look further.
4. **Suggested times** lists up to five slots between 8am and 6pm in the coming week when everyone is free. Click a slot, or click a time in the grid, to move the meeting there.
5. Click **Save**. Each guest gets an invitation by mail.

People outside your organization always show as "Availability unknown", because their calendars can't be seen. Up to 50 people, including you, can be checked at once.

**Guest permissions** decide what guests may do:

- **Modify event**: guests can ask to change the title, place, description or time, and their request is applied automatically.
- **Invite others**: guests can ask to add more guests, who are added automatically.
- **See guest list**: when this is off, each guest's invitation names only them and you.

### Book a room or equipment

In the full form, click **+ Add room/equipment**, search in **Search rooms & equipment…** and pick one. The room is added as a guest and answers like one. Whether it accepts automatically, and how far ahead it can be booked, is set by your administrator.

## Answer an invitation

Invitations arrive as mail. You can answer in either place:

- **In Mail**, the invitation shows the time, place and any conflicts with your calendar. Click **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**. In the message list, an invitation row also has an **RSVP** button with **Accept**, **Decline** and **Maybe**.
- **In Calendar**, open the event and choose under **Your response**.

For a repeating meeting, your answer applies to the whole series. Declining removes the event from your calendar.

**Suggest another time.** In Mail, click **Propose new time**, pick a **Date**, **Start** and **End**, add a comment if you like, and click **Send proposal**.

**Ask for a change.** Open the event and click **Request a change** (or **Add guests**). Change the title, place, time, description or add guests, then click **Send request**. Your calendar changes only when the organizer's update comes back, unless the organizer allowed guests to make that change.

## Change or delete an event

Open the event, then:

- **Modify** opens the full form. For a repeating event, choose under **Apply changes to**: **This event only** or **The entire series**. (Dragging one occurrence of a repeating event always changes only that one.)
- **Delete** asks you to confirm. For a repeating event, choose **Delete this event** or **Delete series**.

Only the organizer can change a meeting's details. If you're a guest, use **Request a change**.

## Reminders

When a reminder is due, a window shows the event title and how soon it starts, wherever you are in the app. **View** opens the event, **Join** or **Open** opens the meeting link if the location is a web address, and **Snooze** reminds you again in 5 minutes. Close the window to dismiss it.

Reminders only show while RapidMX is open in a browser tab. If you turned off **Notifications** in the account menu, reminders are only listed under **Recent notifications**.

## Calendars

- **Add a calendar**: click **+** next to **My calendars** (or next to a mailbox's name), type a name, pick a colour and click **Add**. A calendar's name and colour can't be changed later in the web app, and calendars can't be deleted there yet.
- **Other people's calendars**: when someone shares their mailbox with you, its calendars appear in the list on their own. There is nothing to subscribe to. To share yours, see [Shared mailboxes and delegation](shared-mailboxes.md).
- **Who can see when you're busy**: **Settings** > **Profile** > **Free/busy visibility**. Others only ever see the hours you're busy, never what the event is. See [Settings](settings.md).

## Search your calendar

Type in **Search events** (or press `/`). Matches are found across all the calendars you have ticked. Use **Next match** and **Previous match** (or `Enter` and `Shift+Enter`) to step through them, and **Clear search** to finish.

## Also in the new event window

The window that opens with **+ New event** has tabs:

- **Task** adds a task, with a due date, list, priority, notes and a reminder. See [Tasks](tasks.md).
- **Appointment schedule** (when the booking plugin is installed) creates a page where others can book time with you. See [Booking pages](booking-pages.md).

## Not available yet

There is no printing, no importing or exporting of `.ics` files, and no subscribing to internet calendars in the web app.
